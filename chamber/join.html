NP Membership Benefits

Free membership for registered non-profit organizations serving the Lagos community.

  • Full membership directory listing
  • Invitation to chamber town halls and community outreach programs
  • Quarterly newsletter inclusion
  • Free access to volunteer match events

Bronze Membership Benefits

The entry point for local businesses building their first chamber relationships.

  • Everything in NP Membership
  • Full membership directory listing with industry category
  • Invitation to monthly networking breakfasts
  • 10% discount on chamber workshops and training
  • Monthly chamber newsletter feature

Silver Membership Benefits

For established businesses ready to grow visibility and referral volume.

  • Everything in Bronze Membership
  • Priority seating at chamber headline events
  • Rotating homepage spotlight placement on the chamber site
  • 20% discount on training and advertising packages
  • Two guest passes per member event
  • Listing in the chamber mobile member app

Gold Membership Benefits

Our premier tier for businesses that want to lead the harbor community.

  • Everything in Silver Membership
  • Reserved front-row seating at all chamber events
  • Featured homepage spotlight with a dedicated member story
  • 40% discount on training, advertising and sponsorship packages
  • Four guest passes per member event
  • Named listing in the annual chamber impact report
  • Voting rights on chamber policy and event decisions

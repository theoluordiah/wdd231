Site Plan

WDD 231 Final Project - Oluchukwu Ordiah

Site Name

Lagos Tech Connect

I picked this name because the site helps people in Lagos connect with the tech scene, like communities, events, coworking spaces, and resources.

Domain idea: lagostechconnect.ng

Site Purpose

The site is a guide to the tech community in Lagos for developers and creatives. It will show communities, events, coworking spaces, and resources. Visitors can look through each one, open more details about it, and use a form to suggest something that should be added.

Scenarios

  • I just started learning to code. Which communities in Lagos are good for beginners?
  • Are there any tech events or hackathons happening this month?
  • Where can I find a coworking space with good internet in Yaba?
  • How do I suggest a community that is not on the site?

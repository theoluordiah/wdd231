About Me

I am a software engineer and student pursuing a bachelor's degree in Software Development from Brigham Young University - Idaho. I work as a Backend Engineer, specializing in Node.js, TypeScript, and scalable system design for fintech and productivity tools. My technical foundation includes the Oracle Java Developer Program, NIIT, and the ALX software engineering scholarship.

Beyond programming, I am a videographer and vlogger, documenting my journey as a "tech bro" through my YouTube channel and Instagram. I also give back to the community by volunteering as a developer for Tech4GoodAfrica and serving as a Peer Mentor for ALX Nigeria.
